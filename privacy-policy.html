Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 8 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AstroAskAQuestion collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the Android application and related services.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you sign in, Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication process your Google account information and create a secure authenticated account used to synchronize your data across devices.
  • Profile Information: Information entered by you may include profile name, date of birth, time of birth, birthplace, current city, gender, marital status and other birth related information.
  • Questions and Answers: The astrology questions you submit, the answers returned to you, and related profile identifiers.
  • Purchase Information: Google Play purchase information, purchase tokens and wallet point transactions required for secure billing.
  • Voice Input: When you choose to use voice assistance, Android speech recognition services convert speech into text.
  • Technical Information: Authentication tokens, application logs, service responses, error records and security information.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate your account securely.
  • Create and synchronize your astrology profiles.
  • Generate astrology answers using the information you provide.
  • Maintain your question and answer history.
  • Verify Google Play purchases and manage wallet points.
  • Improve application reliability, security and performance.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Service Providers

AstroAskAQuestion shares information only when required to provide the services you request.

  • Google Sign-In & Firebase Authentication
    Secure user authentication and account synchronization.
  • Google Play Billing
    Purchase verification and wallet management.
  • AstrologyAPI
    Receives only the birth details and question necessary to calculate the requested astrology answer.
  • Secure Hosting Infrastructure
    Stores application data, profiles and answer history.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for third-party advertising purposes.

Storage, Security and Retention

Some information is stored locally on your device. When you sign in, your profiles, wallet information and answer history may also be securely synchronized with our servers so they can be restored on another device.

All communication with our servers uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Although we apply reasonable security measures, no electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud or resolve disputes.

Your Rights

  • Edit or delete individual birth profiles.
  • Delete saved answer history.
  • Permanently delete your account.
  • Revoke microphone permission through Android Settings.

For permanent account deletion instructions, visit the Account Deletion page.

Children

AstroAskAQuestion is intended for adults aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Important Astrology Disclaimer

Astrology is interpretive in nature. Information provided through AstroAskAQuestion is intended for informational and personal guidance purposes only. It should never be considered medical, legal, financial, mental-health, safety or emergency advice.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page together with a revised effective date.
