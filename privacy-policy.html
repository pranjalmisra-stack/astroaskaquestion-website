Privacy Policy for AstroAskAQuestion

Effective date: 8 September 2026

AstroAskAQuestion ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") provides astrology question-and-answer services. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

Information we process

  • Account information: If you sign in, Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication process your Google account information and create an authenticated account identifier used to securely authenticate and synchronize your account across devices.
  • Profile information: Information you enter may include a profile name or nickname, date and time of birth, birthplace, current city or location, gender, marital status, and whether the exact birth time is known.
  • Questions and answers: The astrology questions you submit, the answers returned to you, associated profile identifiers, and information needed to preserve your answer history.
  • Purchase and wallet information: Google Play product, order and purchase-token information needed to verify a purchase, prevent duplicate credits, maintain wallet points, and provide purchased services. We do not receive or store your complete card, bank-account, UPI, or wallet credentials.
  • Voice input: If you tap the microphone and grant permission, Android speech-recognition services process the speech needed to convert it into text. The App does not activate the microphone unless you request voice input.
  • Technical information: Authentication tokens, service responses, error information, and limited technical records necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and prevent abuse of the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users; create and synchronize profiles; standardize locations; send the birth details and question needed to generate an astrology answer; display and preserve answers; verify Google Play purchases; manage wallet points; prevent duplicate or fraudulent transactions; provide account deletion; secure, maintain and improve the service; and comply with applicable law.

Service providers and data sharing

We share information only as needed to operate the App:

  • Google Authentication and Firebase: Google Sign-In, identity-token validation, secure user authentication and account synchronization services.
  • Google Play: in-app purchases, purchase verification and related transaction services.
  • AstrologyAPI: receives the birth/profile details, location information and question required to calculate and return the requested astrology answer.
  • Hosting infrastructure: processes and stores account, profile, answer and wallet records on our behalf.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information or share it for third-party advertising.

Storage, security and retention

Some information is stored locally on your device. When you sign in, profile, answer-history and wallet information may also be stored by our backend under your authenticated account so it can be restored or synchronized. Information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. We use reasonable safeguards, but no electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

Account and profile information is retained while needed to provide the service or until you delete it, subject to backup, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, transaction, tax, accounting, legal and security retention requirements. Purchase records may be retained where necessary to prevent duplicate credits and meet legal obligations.

Your controls

You can edit or delete individual profiles and clear answer history in the App. You can also use the App's permanent account-deletion option to request deletion of account-associated cloud data. Instructions are available on our account deletion page. You may revoke microphone permission through Android settings.

Children

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Important astrology notice

Astrology is interpretive. App content should not be treated as certainty or as professional medical, legal, financial, mental-health, safety, or emergency advice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or our legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will be updated when changes are published.
