Privacy Policy for AstroAskAQuestion

Effective date: 9 September 2026

AstroAskAQuestion ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") provides astrology question-and-answer services. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, why it is processed, how it is handled, and the choices available to you.

Information We Process

  • Account information: If you sign in, Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication process information needed to authenticate your Google account and create an authenticated account identifier used to securely synchronize your AstroAskAQuestion account across supported devices.
  • Profile information: Information you enter may include a profile name or nickname, date and time of birth, birthplace, current city or location, gender, marital status, and other information required to create and use a birth profile.
  • Questions and answers: We process the astrology questions you submit, the answers returned to you, associated profile identifiers, and information required to preserve your answer history.
  • Purchase and wallet information: Google Play product information, order information and purchase-token information may be processed to verify purchases, prevent duplicate credits, maintain wallet points, and provide purchased services. We do not receive or store your complete payment-card number, bank-account credentials, UPI credentials, or digital-wallet credentials.
  • Voice input: If you tap the microphone and grant the required permission, Android speech-recognition services process the speech required to convert your spoken input into text. The App does not intentionally activate voice input unless you request it.
  • Technical information: Authentication tokens, service responses, error information, request identifiers, and limited technical records may be processed when necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot and protect the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users; create and synchronize profiles; standardize location information; send required birth details and questions for astrology processing; display and preserve answers; verify Google Play purchases; maintain wallet points; prevent duplicate or fraudulent transactions; recover eligible answers after interrupted connections; provide account deletion; secure and maintain the service; provide support; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Service Providers and Data Sharing

Information is shared only where reasonably necessary to operate AstroAskAQuestion.

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase: Used for authentication, identity-token validation, secure account identification, cloud services and synchronization.
  • Google Play: Used for in-app purchases, purchase verification, transaction processing and related Google Play services.
  • AstrologyAPI: Receives information required to process an astrology request, which may include relevant birth/profile details, location information and the astrology question submitted by the user.
  • Hosting and cloud infrastructure: Used to process or store account, profile, answer, recovery, application and wallet-related records on our behalf.

These service providers process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information or share personal information for third-party advertising.

Storage, Security and Retention

Some application information may be stored locally on your device. When you sign in, profile information, answer history, wallet information and related account records may also be stored through our backend and cloud services so that eligible information can be synchronized or restored.

Information transmitted between the App and our backend is protected in transit using HTTPS. We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards, but no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Account and profile information is retained while required to provide the service or until it is deleted, subject to legitimate backup, transaction, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, accounting, tax, legal and security requirements.

Certain purchase or transaction records may be retained where reasonably necessary to prevent duplicate credits, investigate fraud, maintain transaction integrity or comply with applicable obligations.

Your Controls

You can manage and delete individual profiles within the App. You can also manage eligible saved answer history and permanently delete your cloud account using the account-deletion controls provided within AstroAskAQuestion.

Detailed instructions are available on our Account Deletion page.

You can revoke microphone permission at any time through your Android device settings.

Children

AstroAskAQuestion is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Important Astrology Notice

Astrology is interpretive. Information provided through AstroAskAQuestion should not be treated as certainty or as professional medical, legal, financial, mental-health, safety or emergency advice.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our services, our service providers, or applicable requirements change. When material changes are published, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
