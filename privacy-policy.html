Privacy Policy

Introduction

AstroAskAQuestion respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

  • Birth details entered by you.
  • Profile information that you choose to save.
  • Google Sign-In information used for authentication.
  • Wallet balance and purchase history.
  • Astrology questions submitted by you.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide Vedic astrology question and answer services.
  • To maintain your saved profiles.
  • To synchronize your wallet and previous answers across devices.
  • To improve the application and customer support.

Payments

Payments are processed through Google Play Billing. AstroAskAQuestion does not store your payment card information.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures are used to protect user information.

Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your cloud account directly from within the AstroAskAQuestion Android application.
