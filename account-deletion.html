Delete Your AstroAskAQuestion Account and Data

AstroAskAQuestion allows you to delete individual profile information without deleting your complete account, or permanently delete your AstroAskAQuestion cloud account and associated application data.

Delete a Profile Without Deleting Your Account

  1. Open AstroAskAQuestion and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile screen.
  3. Select the birth profile you want to remove.
  4. Choose the profile deletion option.
  5. Review the confirmation message and confirm the deletion.

Deleting an individual profile does not automatically delete your entire AstroAskAQuestion account.

Manage Saved Answer History

Where the App provides an option to clear eligible saved answer history, you may use that option to remove the selected question-and-answer history without deleting your complete account.

Permanently Delete Your Account Through the App

  1. Open AstroAskAQuestion.
  2. Sign in using the Google account associated with your AstroAskAQuestion account.
  3. Open the Ask Your Question screen.
  4. Select DELETE MY ACCOUNT FROM CLOUD PERMANENTLY.
  5. Carefully review the warning displayed by the App.
  6. Confirm the permanent account deletion.
Important: Permanent account deletion may remove cloud information that cannot subsequently be restored through AstroAskAQuestion.

If You Cannot Access the App

If you cannot access AstroAskAQuestion but need assistance with an account deletion request, contact:

astroaskaquestion.support@gmail.com

Use the subject: AstroAskAQuestion Account Deletion Request.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, complete payment-card information, UPI credentials, Google Play purchase tokens, government identification documents or other highly sensitive credentials by email.

We may require an appropriate secure method to verify that the deletion request relates to the account concerned.

Data Deleted

Subject to successful processing of the deletion request, AstroAskAQuestion may delete active account-associated records such as:

  • Cloud account-related application records
  • Cloud-synchronized birth profiles
  • Cloud-synchronized answer history
  • Wallet balance associated with the AstroAskAQuestion account
  • Pending answer or recovery records linked to the account
  • Other active account-linked application records

Information stored locally on your Android device may separately be removed by clearing the App's local storage or uninstalling the App.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain records may be retained where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, transaction integrity, security, accounting, tax, dispute resolution or legal requirements.

Completed Google Play purchase records may also remain within Google Play according to Google's own policies and applicable retention requirements.

Firebase, Google and other service providers may independently retain information according to their own applicable terms, policies and legal obligations.
