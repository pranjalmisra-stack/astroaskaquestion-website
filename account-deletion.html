Account Deletion

Delete your AstroAskAQuestion Account and Data

AstroAskAQuestion lets you delete individual profile data without deleting your account, or permanently delete your cloud account and associated synchronized data.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  1. Open AstroAskAQuestion and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile screen.
  3. Select the profile you want to delete.
  4. Delete only that profile if you no longer need it.
  5. Open Past Answers if you wish to remove saved answer history.
  6. Confirm the deletion inside the App.

Deleting a profile removes that profile's birth details and associated synchronized history. Deleting answer history removes only the selected saved answers. These actions do not delete your AstroAskAQuestion account.

Permanently delete your account

  1. Open AstroAskAQuestion.
  2. Sign in using the Google account associated with your AstroAskAQuestion account.
  3. Open the Ask Your Question screen.
  4. Select Delete My Account Permanently from Cloud.
  5. Read the warning carefully.
  6. Confirm the permanent deletion.

If you cannot access the App

Email pranjal.misra@gmail.com with the subject AstroAskAQuestion Account Deletion Request. Please do not include passwords, OTPs, payment card details, UPI information, or Google Play purchase tokens in your email.

Data deleted

Deleting your account removes:

  • Cloud account information
  • Saved birth profiles
  • Wallet balance
  • Cloud synchronized answer history
  • Pending answers
  • Other account-associated application records

Local data stored on your Android device may also be removed by clearing the App storage or uninstalling the App.

Data that may be retained

Completed Google Play purchase records, fraud-prevention records, security logs, tax, accounting, and legal records may be retained where required by applicable law or operational necessity. Google Play and Firebase may also retain information according to their own privacy policies.
