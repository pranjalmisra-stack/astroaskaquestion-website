Delete your AstroAskAQuestion account and data

AstroAskAQuestion lets you delete individual profile data without deleting your account, or permanently delete your account and its associated cloud data.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  1. Open AstroAskAQuestion and sign in.
  2. Open the profile-management screen.
  3. To remove one birth profile, select that profile and choose the profile deletion option.
  4. To remove saved answers for a profile, open Past Answers and choose the clear-history option.
  5. Confirm the deletion shown in the App.

Deleting a profile removes that profile's birth details and associated synchronized history. Clearing answer history removes the saved question-and-answer history selected in the App. These actions do not require deletion of the entire account.

Delete through the App

  1. Open AstroAskAQuestion.
  2. Sign in using the phone number associated with the account.
  3. Open the account or profile-management screen.
  4. Select Delete account permanently.
  5. Review the warning and confirm deletion.

If you cannot access the App

Email pranjal.misra@gmail.com with the subject AstroAskAQuestion account deletion request. Do not email identity documents, passwords, OTPs, complete payment-card details, or Google Play purchase tokens. We may ask you to verify control of the account through an appropriate secure method before deletion.

Data deleted

The deletion process removes account wallet information, cloud-synchronized profiles and answer histories, pending answers, and other account-linked application records from the active service. Local App data may be removed by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

Data that may be retained

Limited purchase, fraud-prevention, security, dispute, tax, accounting or legal records may be retained where required or reasonably necessary. Where appropriate, retained operational records are disassociated from the deleted account. Google Play and Firebase may retain information under their own policies and legal obligations.
