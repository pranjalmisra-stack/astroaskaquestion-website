About AstroAskAQuestion

AstroAskAQuestion is an Android application designed to make Vedic astrology guidance simple, accessible and easy to use.

Users can create private birth profiles, enter their birth details, ask astrology questions and maintain separate answer histories for different profiles.

The App includes Google Sign-In, cloud synchronization, voice assistance and support for multiple Indian languages.

App Features

Vedic Astrology Questions

Ask astrology questions using your saved birth details.

Multiple Profiles

Create and maintain separate private birth profiles.

Google Sign-In

Secure authentication and account synchronization.

Google Play Billing

Purchase wallet points securely through Google Play.

Voice Assistance

Use voice input to make data entry and question submission easier.

Indian Languages

Designed to support users in multiple Indian languages.

Private Answer History

Keep astrology questions and answers associated with the correct profile.

Cloud Restore

Restore eligible profiles and answer history after signing in.

How It Works

1. Create a Profile

Enter the birth details required for the astrology profile.

2. Ask a Question

Type or speak your astrology question.

3. Receive Your Answer

Your astrology answer is displayed in the App.

4. Save Your History

Review previous questions and answers from your saved history.
